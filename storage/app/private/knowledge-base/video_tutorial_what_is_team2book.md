\f0\fs24 \cf0 # What is Team2Book?\
\
## Overview\
\
Team2Book is a resource planning solution specialized for clinics and healthcare organizations.\
\
Many organizations face a common challenge: they often have more healthcare professionals than available rooms. Managing room assignments and creating schedules manually quickly becomes time-consuming and complex.\
\
Team2Book automates the room assignment process, allowing managers to spend less time on repetitive scheduling tasks and more time supporting their teams and patients.\
\
---\
\
# How Team2Book Works\
\
Team2Book integrates with Teamup calendars.\
\
Team members simply enter their availability or unavailability into their Teamup calendars.\
\
The Team2Book planning engine analyzes this information and automatically assigns rooms to each available professional while considering multiple scheduling rules, including:\
\
- Team member priorities\
- Resource preferences (for example, a physician who prefers a specific office)\
- Reserved resources\
- Special events such as evening or weekend clinics\
- Resource reservations based on categories\
\
Once the planning process is complete, Team2Book automatically writes the room assignments directly into the Teamup resource calendars.\
\
Healthcare professionals can then view their assigned room at any time using either their computer or mobile device.\
\

# Main Benefits\
\
## Automation\
\
Team2Book automatically sends monthly reminder emails asking team members to submit their availability.\
\
This eliminates the need for managers to manually request schedules every month.\
\
## Fairness\
\
Room assignments are performed objectively according to the scheduling rules defined by management.\
\
This ensures a transparent and consistent allocation process.\
\
## Flexibility\
\
Last-minute changes are easy to manage.\
\
If someone updates their availability, Team2Book recalculates the schedule and updates room assignments within minutes.\
\
## Centralization\
\
All availability, schedules, and room assignments are stored in one centralized location that is accessible to the entire team.\
\
## Connectivity\
\
Team members can synchronize their Team2Book work schedule with their personal calendar, including:\
\
- Google Calendar\
- Apple Calendar\
- Microsoft Outlook\
\

# Summary\
\
Team2Book simplifies resource planning for clinics and healthcare organizations.\
\
By automatically assigning available professionals to available rooms, it reduces scheduling conflicts, saves managers significant administrative time, and provides healthcare professionals with immediate access to their room assignments.\
\
The result is a more efficient, fair, and flexible scheduling process.\

# Frequently Asked Questions\
\
## What is Team2Book?\
\
Team2Book is a resource planning application that automatically assigns rooms and other resources to healthcare professionals based on their availability and scheduling rules.\
\
## Who is Team2Book designed for?\
\
Team2Book is designed primarily for clinics, medical practices, healthcare centers, and organizations that need to manage shared rooms or other resources.\
\
## Does Team2Book replace Teamup?\
\
No.\
\
Team2Book works together with Teamup. Teamup is used for entering availability and displaying calendars, while Team2Book performs the automated planning and room assignment.\
\
## How are room assignments determined?\
\
Assignments are calculated automatically using configurable business rules such as:\
\
- Professional priorities\
- Resource preferences\
- Reserved rooms\
- Special events\
- Resource categories\
\
## Can Team2Book handle last-minute schedule changes?\
\
Yes.\
\
When availability changes, Team2Book can quickly regenerate assignments so schedules remain up to date.\
\
## Can staff view their assigned room from a mobile phone?\
\
Yes.\
\
Assignments are available from both desktop computers and mobile devices through the Teamup calendars.\
\
## Which calendars can Team2Book synchronize with?\
\
Team members can synchronize their schedules with:\
\
- Google Calendar\
- Apple Calendar\
- Microsoft Outlook\
\
## What are the main advantages of Team2Book?\
\
The primary benefits include:\
\
- Automated scheduling workflows\
- Fair and objective room assignments\
- Rapid updates after schedule changes\
- Centralized scheduling information\
- Calendar synchronization with personal calendars\
\
## What problem does Team2Book solve?\
\
Team2Book solves the challenge of assigning limited rooms and shared resources among many professionals while minimizing manual work and scheduling conflicts.}
