**Description**
---------------

You may be working with clinical assistants or licensed practical nurses. Often, their role is to help the physician by pairing with their clinical activities.

[Team2Book](https://team2book.com) can now propose an “**automatic**” and “**fair**” assignment of your clinical assistants.

You can also view the historical and upcoming distribution of [licensed practical nurses](https://www.quebec.ca/en/employment/learn-trade-occupation/exploring-trades-occupations/32101-licensed-practical-nurses) through the “**Assistants**” tab in the “**Statistics**” section of your Team2Book dashboard.

This short tutorial shows you how to use this feature.

---

**To automatically assign your assistants**
-------------------------------------------

Team2Book relies on the assignment of the last 3 months to attempt to distribute your assistants fairly.

1. Go to the **“Scheduler”** section of your Team2Book dashboard.

<figure class="wp-block-image size-full is-resized">![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20600%20250'%3E%3C/svg%3E)<noscript>![](https://team2book.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/scheduler.png)</noscript></figure>2. Choose the “**Assign assistants**” tab, which will now appear at the top of the page.

<figure class="wp-block-image size-full is-resized">![Automatic assignment of assistants.  Button](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20400%20175'%3E%3C/svg%3E)<noscript>![Automatic assignment of assistants.  Button](https://team2book.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/assignassistants.png)</noscript></figure>3. Select the start and end dates for the assignment.
4. Select the assistant group(s).
5. Click “**Assign**”.

<figure class="wp-block-image size-full is-resized">![Result of the Automatic assignment of assistants](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20800%20600'%3E%3C/svg%3E)<noscript>![Result of the Automatic assignment of assistants](https://team2book.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/assignassistants2.png)</noscript></figure>Team2Book will proceed as follows:

- For each available consumer who must be assisted during the chosen period, Team2Book will try to find an available assistant.
- Team2Book will populate the assistant’s **“Teamed With”** field for the duration of the assistance period.
- A summary table will show the assignment results. Failed assignments (i.e., no assistant found) will also be listed.
- At the next scheduler run, the resource assignments for assistants and assisted consumers will populate the **“Teamed With”** field.

**Important notes:**

- The assisted consumer must be available.
- The assistant must be available.
- Team2Book will replace all existing availabilities during the assisted period. (**Proceed with caution!**).
- Team2Book will split assistants’ availabilities if the availability exceeds the assistance period. 
    - Example: If an assistant is available all day but must assist only in the morning, Team2Book will split the availability into 2 assistant events: 
        1. A morning event with the “**Teamed With**” field filled
        2. An afternoon event **without** the “**Teamed With**” field
- Team2Book will split the assisted consumers’ availabilities if they exceed the assistance period. 
    - This way, the consumer knows when an assistant is working with them and when they are working alone.
- Assistant assignment also works for special events.
- A [notification](https://team2book.com/tutorials/notifications-module) will be sent to the team manager if Team2Book cannot find any assistant available to assist a consumer.
- For now, no error message will remain displayed in the “**Scheduler**” section of your Team2Book dashboard.
- If an assistant is only partially available, Team2Book will assign them and indicate it in the summary table (Coverage column: “**Full**” or “**Partial**”).

**Statistics**
--------------

To verify whether assistant assignments are fair, take a look at the [statistics](https://team2book.com/tutorials/statistics-module)!

1. Go to the **“Statistics”** section of your Team2Book dashboard.
2. Select the **“Assistants”** tab at the top of the page.
3. Choose the desired parameters.
