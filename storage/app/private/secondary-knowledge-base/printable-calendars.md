You can [modify](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/calendar-link-parameters/#using-calendar-link-parameters) these parameters to adjust how the blank calendar appears before printing or saving.
- **[Calendar views](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-calendar-views/)**: Teamup has many unique and adjustable calendar views. Switch to another view in the top right-hand corner. Adjust the [settings](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-calendar-views/#specific-settings-for-different-calendar-views) and [calendar resolution](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-calendar-views/#calendar-resolution) for the best display for your purposes.

You can print consecutive date ranges for a set of blank calendars or planners.

#### <span class="ez-toc-section" id="use-your-own-teamup-calendar-with-or-without-your-brand-color"></span>**Use your own Teamup calendar with or without your brand color:**<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span>

- Open your Teamup calendar as usual in a web browser. Append *?showHeader=0* to your calendar URL in the address bar to remove the entire header, or *showProfileAndInfo=0* to only remove unwanted info but keep your calendar title and header color. Learn more about [link parameters](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/calendar-link-parameters/).
- Click the eye icon above the calendar list to [hide](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/hide-calendars/) all sub-calendars. This will display a blank calendar. Close the side panel, then choose the calendar view, date range or [resolution](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/change-time-resolution-grid-views/). When ready, simply Ctrl-P (on Windows) or Cmd-P (on Mac) to print or save as a PDF.
