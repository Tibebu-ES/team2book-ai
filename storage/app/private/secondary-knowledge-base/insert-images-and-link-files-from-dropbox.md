 </nav> </div><div class="hkb-article__content" itemprop="text">You can insert images or file links into Teamup calendar events directly from your Dropbox account. To learn about inserting or uploading images from other services, see [this article](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-attach-files-to-events/).

### Insert a file from Dropbox

1. Open the event.
2. Scroll to the field where you wish to insert the image: 
    - In the Description field, **select the Dropbox icon:**   
        [![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/tu-insert-image-dropbox-1024x176.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/tu-insert-image-dropbox.png)
    - In the Attachments field, **click** **Upload**, then **select Dropbox from the left side**.
    - In the Event Comments field, **click Add Comment**, then **select the Dropbox icon**.
3. If not logged into Dropbox, you will be prompted to do so.
4. From your Dropbox folder, **choose the image or file:** [![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/dropbox-2.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/dropbox-2.png)
5. **Save** the event.

### Resize Dropbox images in your Teamup Calendar

By default, the image will be displayed **in its original size,** which may be too large for your calendar in many cases.

- **Resize the image by clicking on a corner tab of the image and dragging** inwardly to reduce the size.

You can also modify image details in the image editor:

- After the image is uploaded, click on it.
- Then click the image icon to open the *Insert/Edit Image* window.
- In the *General* tab, you can add an image description, if desired:

<div class="wp-block-image"><figure class="aligncenter">[![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/dropbox-3.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/dropbox-3.png)</figure></div>  
Select the *Advanced* tab to apply additional customization:

- Enter numbers in the boxes to adjust vertical or horizontal space around the image. The measurement is in pixels.
- Enter a number in the border box to add a box; the width of the border is measured in pixels. If you enter the number 5 in the border box, a border with a width of 5 pixels will be added to your image.

<div class="wp-block-image"><figure class="aligncenter">[![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/dropbox-4.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/dropbox-4.png)</figure></div>### Good to know

- When inserting a file to your calendar event from Dropbox, **a link to your Dropbox file** is inserted into your event. If you move the image or file to a different Dropbox location, or remove it from Dropbox, the link will be broken.
- Teamup Calendar also allows you to upload images and documents directly into events as [explained here](http://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-attach-files-to-events/).
