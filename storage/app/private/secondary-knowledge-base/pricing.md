If your organization requires any of the following, an organization account is needed:

- Support for [Single Sign On](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/single-sign-on-with-teamup/) (SSO)
- Flexible, scalable user management (for example, providing read-only access to a large number of users for a company-wide event calendar)
- Centralized administration and consolidated billing for multiple master calendars, each subscribed to one of the plans listed above

Teamup organization accounts are designed to improve schedule visibility, security, and operational efficiency across multiple teams within your organization.

Pricing for organization accounts starts at US$1,200 per year and scales incrementally according to the number of users.

For more details or to discuss your needs, please contact us with the following information:

- A brief description of your primary use case or business context
- Your specific goals or jobs-to-be-done
- The total number of users across your organization who need access to Teamup calendars
- Whether SSO is required
