<span>**Get started:** Create a </span>[free user account](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-are-user-accounts/)<span> and login. On your [dashboard](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/add-calendar-to-dashboard/), tap a calendar title to open it. If you have a [shareable calendar link](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-tell-if-your-calendar-access-is-account-based-or-a-link/#link-based-calendar-url), you can </span>[add it to your dashboard](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/add-a-calendar-to-your-dashboard-in-a-browser/)<span> or simply open the link without login.</span>

<span>**Get the app:** Download the Teamup app ([iOS](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/teamup-calendar/id1065897968) or [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.teamup.teamup)) and [log in to access](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/teamup-app/#get-started) your calendars on a mobile device. Stay synced automatically across devices.</span>

<span><span aria-label="" class="c-mrkdwn__br" data-stringify-type="paragraph-break"></span>**Share a Teamup calendar:** The [calendar administrator](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/getting-started-guide-for-administrators/) can set up [customized calendar access](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-share-your-teamup-calendar-users-groups-links/) for users, or use a [secure calendar link](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-embed-the-same-calendar-in-multiple-places/#share-a-secure-link) to share a calendar publicly or [embed Teamup](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/embed-teamup-calendar/) on a website..</span>

### <span class="ez-toc-section" id="contact-teamup-support"></span>Contact Teamup Support<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span>

Before contacting our support desk, please do [check out our Knowledge Base. ](https://calendar.teamup.com/)

For more help, email us:<span> </span><support@teamup.com>. For the best support, describe your needs and questions in detail. If you need assistance with setup, consider including the following information:

- What area of your business will Teamup be used for?
- What kind of information will be organized on the calendar?
- How many [sub-calendars](http://calendar.teamup.com/kb/color-coded-calendars/) (categories) do you need?
- Will you need [custom fields](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/event-fields/#custom-event-fields)?
- How many people will access the calendar?
- Who will the users be, and what will they need to do with the calendar?
- What time range and [calendar view ](http://calendar.teamup.com/kb/video-configure-calendar-views/)will work best for your team?
- What level of [security](http://calendar.teamup.com/kb/secure-teamup-calendar/) are you comfortable with?
- Would you like to be able to [upload files](http://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-attach-files-to-events/)?
- Would users need a [Daily Agenda](http://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-teamup-daily-agenda/) or other [notifications](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/managing-notifications/)?
- Any other requirements or wishes?

If you have emailed us and did not receive a reply, please check your spam or junk mail folder.

Please note that we are able to provide support via email only. Generally we do not provide phone consultation unless we understand the context of your case and believe a phone call is necessary.
