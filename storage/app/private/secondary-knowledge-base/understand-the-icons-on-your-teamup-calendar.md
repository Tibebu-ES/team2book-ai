<meta content="April 4, 2022" itemprop="datePublished"></meta> <header class="hkb-article__header">Understand the Icons on Your Teamup Calendar
============================================

 </header></head><body><div class="hkb-article__content" itemprop="text">Ever wondered what the little event symbols that you see in your Teamup Calendar mean?

![4 icons in an event bubble .jpg](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![4 icons in an event bubble .jpg](https://s3-eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/upload.teamup.com/473180/QLvTVV12S9OAOPyzTULq_4-20icons-20in-20an-20event-20bubble-20.jpg)</noscript>

From left to right, here is an explanation:

1. **Flag:** The flag icon shows on events that have open [signups](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/signup-for-an-event/). Once the signup maximum has been reached or the signup deadline has passed, the flag will disappear; this means that signups for that event are closed.
2. **Speech bubble:** The speech bubble icon indicates that the event has [comments](https://blog.teamup.com/2018/07/16/add-comments-to-events/).
3. **Two arrows in a circle:** The recurrence symbol shows that an event is part of a recurring series, i.e., it is set to [repeat](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/recurring-events/).
4. **Two overlapping squares:** This icon means that the event is assigned to [more than one sub-calendar](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/assign-one-event-to-multiple-calendars/).

Another place that you may see a symbol is in your list of [sub-calendars](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-is-a-sub-calendar/):

![Public Holiday sub-calendar list.jpgs.jpg](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![Public Holiday sub-calendar list.jpgs.jpg](https://s3-eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/upload.teamup.com/473180/tvl4gChQt6ngU7Vb5LRI_Public-20Holiday-20sub-calendar-20list.jpgs.jpg)</noscript>

The **lock** indicates that events on those sub-calendars will be read-only. You will also see the lock icon on individual events that are read-only, e.g. all events from an [inbound iCalendar feed](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/icalendar-feeds-inbound/) display the lock icon.
