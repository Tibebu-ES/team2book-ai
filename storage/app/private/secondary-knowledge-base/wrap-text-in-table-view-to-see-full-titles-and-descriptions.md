<meta content="May 10, 2022" itemprop="datePublished"></meta> <header class="hkb-article__header">Wrap Text in Table View to See Full Titles and Descriptions
===========================================================

 </header></head><body><div class="hkb-article__content" itemprop="text">Event title wrapping is supported in the Scheduler, Day, Multi Day and Week [views](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-calendar-views/), but not in the monthly or multi-week view. These views show each event bubble as one line, thus titles may be cut off. You may also wish to easily see the full description of events for several weeks at a time.

Table view makes it easy! You can try it yourself on our [live demo calendar](https://teamup.com/ksmxbfc4qf2cwrswxe).

Just set the date range, then click the box to *Wrap content*:
