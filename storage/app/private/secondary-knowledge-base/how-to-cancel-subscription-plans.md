Cancel a subscription
---------------------

1. Open the calendar in a browser.
2. Click the **blue menu** (top right).
3. Select **Settings**.
4. Go to the **Subscription tab** in Settings.
5. Click **Cancel Subscription** in the top right:[![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/cancel.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/cancel.png)
6. On the next screen, confirm by clicking the red **Cancel Subscription button**:[![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/tu-subscription-cancel2-1024x426.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/tu-subscription-cancel2.png)

Your subscription will remain active through the end of your paid period, then it will be downgraded automatically to the free plan at the end of your current billing period. [Read more](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/upgrade-and-downgrade-subscription-plan-in-the-new-billing-system/#cancel-your-subscription) about managing your subscription.

You can use Teamup on the free plan as long as you would like. It is provided as a [free service](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-teamup-as-a-free-calendar-service/).

**⚠️ Canceling a subscription does not delete the calendar. If you want to permanently remove a calendar, see [How to Delete an Entire Calendar](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/delete-calendars/).**

About Teamup subscription plans
-------------------------------

Teamup’s [subscription plans](https://www.teamup.com/pricing/) are not based on user accounts, which is always free for anyone. Instead, they are [calendar-based](https://teamup.com/event/show/id/U6zjMotpg5iNKZBuottajcWK4D69ZW).

Each subscription plan is bound to a specific calendar. Each plan must be cancelled within the respective calendar.

Calendar-based administration, including subscriptions and calendar access permissions, allows centralized management. It’s better for broader collaboration among individual users who share the use of the calendar. It’s also a better method when there is organizational change, e.g. a new [calendar administrator](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-is-the-calendar-administrator/) takes over.

To prevent your subscription from being renewed automatically, make sure to cancel it before the end of your paid billing period.
