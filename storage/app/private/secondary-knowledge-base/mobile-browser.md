Open Teamup on a mobile browser
-------------------------------

### Open from the Teamup app

[![Three mobile phone screens show the Teamup app: Left sidebar with a gear icon in the top right; App settings with Calendar URL highlighted; App settings with Open calendar in browser option highlighted.](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![Three mobile phone screens show the Teamup app: Left sidebar with a gear icon in the top right; App settings with Calendar URL highlighted; App settings with Open calendar in browser option highlighted.](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/240910tum-app-open-mobile-browser-1024x576.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/240910tum-app-open-mobile-browser.png)

To open a calendar in a mobile browser from the Teamup app:

1. Swipe or tap to open the **left sidebar**.
2. Tap the **gear ⚙ icon** to access app settings.
3. Tap **Calendar URL**, then tap **Open calendar in browser**.

You can also get the calendar URL from the dashboard:

1. Swipe or tap to open the **right sidebar**.
2. Tap **Dashboard**.
3. Find the calendar on your dashboard and click the **gear ⚙ icon**.
4. Tap **Copy calendar URL**.
5. Open a mobile browser and paste the link into the address bar.

### Open a calendar link

To open a calendar link directly:

Option 1: If the link is in an email or text message, **tap to open it** on your device.

- - You may be prompted to choose which mobile browser you want to use.
    - If you have the Teamup app installed, your device will usually open the link in the app by default. To avoid this, long press to open a context menu and copy the link. Then continue with Option 2.

Option 2: Open your mobile browser, then **type or paste the link** into the address bar.

Note: If you open an account-based calendar URL, you’ll be prompted to log in on the mobile browser.

Use Teamup on a mobile browser
------------------------------

### Reasons to use a mobile browser

- You want the same look and feel of the calendar on your mobile device as on the desktop.
- You are most comfortable with using Teamup in ways that are consistent on both mobile and desktop.
- You received a Teamup calendar link but would prefer not to set it up in the app.
- You want to access the calendar settings, which are not accessible from the Teamup app.
- You want to save an event to the default calendar application on your device, e.g. iCal on an iPhone.
- You want to access Teamup on a tablet/iPad with the full web client experience.

### Tips for using Teamup in a mobile browser

In a mobile browser, you can interact with Teamup as you would on a laptop or desktop computer. All features and functions work in the same way. However, the smaller screen size can make calendar use a bit different.

- Experiment a bit to find the best way to view your calendar on the small screen. Day, List, Agenda, and Scheduler view tend to work well on smaller screens.
- You can create and modify events, access settings, use event signups and comments, and upload files or images when using a mobile browser.
- Make use of the **[Sharing options](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/share-events-customize-sharing-options/)** that allow you to save the event to your personal calendar or to share further via social media apps, email, or as a web page.

#### Add a calendar link to your home screen

[![Four mobile phone screens show the steps to add a desktop shortcut to an Android device](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![Four mobile phone screens show the steps to add a desktop shortcut to an Android device](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Android-home-screen-1024x395.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Android-home-screen.png)

Make it easy to open a calendar in your mobile browser anytime by creating a shortcut. Depending on your device, these instructions may vary:

- Open the calendar in your mobile browser.
- Tap the Share menu and select **Add to Home Screen**, OR
- Tap the menu button in your browser and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
- After creating the shortcut, tap the Teamup icon to access your calendar.

[![Add a Teamup calendar shortcut to your iOS device homescreen](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![Add a Teamup calendar shortcut to your iOS device homescreen](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/iosbrowsermockup2.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/iosbrowsermockup2.png)

You can add multiple calendar links to the home screen, resulting in multiple Teamup shortcuts. Give each shortcut a different name to differentiate between them. Your Teamup shortcuts can be placed side by side on your home screen or organized in a Teamup folder, if you prefer.

Note: **Shortcut icons show the Teamup logo with a white border**, whereas **the Teamup app icon has a green border**.

#### Return to the Teamup app from a mobile browser

You can alternate between viewing your calendar in the Teamup app and a mobile browser on your device. If you have the Teamup app installed, your mobile browser [may show an app banner](https://teamup.com/event/show/id/CsTYNqtAno8pzPZr5TEQJR5m2vgmVX) above the calendar.

To return to the app, tap the button or link in the banner.
