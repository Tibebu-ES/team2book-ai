Patient Satisfaction
--------------------

Patient satisfaction is usually the focal point of any healthcare facility. Therefore, the performance of an administrator can be determined by the following:

### Complaint Volume

Efficient communication, reduced wait times, and improved scheduling decrease the overall patient complaints, which underlines an administrator’s competence.

### Staff Punctuality

Effective management from administrators ensures doctors, nurses, and other workers go to work on time.

### Test Results Follow-up Management

A good administrator ensures that all tests results are delivered to the right health care professionnal in a timely and accurate manner.

### Paperwork Management

A competent manager streamlines paperwork processing, reducing the likely problems patients may face from their insurers.

### Clinic Availability and Service Expansion

Visionary administrators are always looking to increase the availability of healthcare professionals. Similarly, they look for ways to provide a diverse set of services that cater to different patients.

By extension, improved availability and service expansion should enhance accessibility to healthcare services.

Objective-based Performance Evaluation
--------------------------------------

[Key performance indicators (KPIs)](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/k/kpi.asp) can help evaluate the performance of administrators. These KPIs include:

- Doctor satisfaction surveys;
- Technology adaptation success rate;
- Operational issue resolution rate;
- Healthcare facility occupancy rates.

On the other hand, incentives like salary increases or bonuses can encourage managers to continuously seek to enhance the overall effectiveness and efficiency of a hospital or clinic.
