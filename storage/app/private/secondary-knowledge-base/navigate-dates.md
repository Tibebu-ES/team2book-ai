 </nav> </div><div class="hkb-article__content" itemprop="text">Navigating date ranges or getting to a specific date is fast and easy on Teamup. Here’s how to move forward or back over date ranges or jump to a specific date in a browser.

Move through date ranges
------------------------

[![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/250520tu-navigate-date-range-today-button.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/250520tu-navigate-date-range-today-button.png)

**Move through consecutive date ranges** with the arrows next to the Today button:

- Click the left arrow to move to the previous date range.
- Click the right arrow to move to the next date range.
- Click Today anytime to return to the current date.

**The date range adjusts automatically** depending on the [calendar view.](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-are-calendar-views/)

[![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/1744913985276.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/1744913985276.png)

- Year view will move one year at at time (or the number of months displayed).
- Month view will move one month at a time.
- Week view will move one week at a time, etc.

Jump to a specific date
-----------------------

[![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/250520tu-both-date-pickers.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/250520tu-both-date-pickers.png)

**Jump to a specific date** using the [date pickers](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-is-the-date-picker/):

- Open the [control panel](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/hiding-showing-calendars-left-control-panel/) to access the date picker there, or click the arrow beside the date indicator to open the dropdown date picker.
- Click on a date to jump to it.
- Use the arrows to move through months.
- Scroll on a trackpad or mouse wheel to quickly move through months.

Date controls in calendar views
-------------------------------

Some [calendar views](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-calendar-views/#12-calendar-views-browser) have specific options for navigating dates and date ranges.

- [Agenda view](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-calendar-views/#3-list-and-agenda-views): Adjust the date range.
- [List view](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-calendar-views/#3-list-and-agenda-views): Adjust the date range and event grouping.
- [Scheduler view](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/scheduler-view/#how-to-use-scheduler-view): Adjust the resolution and date range.
- [Table view](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/the-table-view/#adjust-date-range-and-columns): Adjust the date range.
- [Timeline view](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/the-timeline-view/#adjust-date-range-and-zoom-level): Adjust the date range and zoom level.
- [Tiles view](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/the-tiles-view/#how-to-use-tiles-view): Adjust the date range and event grouping.
- [Year view](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/the-year-view/#adjust-visible-months): Adjust the visible months.

The calendar administrator can [configure](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-calendar-views/#specific-settings-for-different-calendar-views) the number of days for Multi-day view and the number of weeks for Multi-week view.
