===============================================

 </header></head><body><div class="ht-mobile-toc">**Contents** <nav id="navtoc" role="navigation">1. [Toggle to hide or show sub-calendars](#toggle-to-hide-or-show-sub-calendars)
2. [Filter by sub-calendars ](#filter-by-sub-calendars)

 </nav> </div><div class="hkb-article__content" itemprop="text">When you have many events on your screen and you need to see only the events on a specific sub-calendar, there are two ways to achieve this:

#### Toggle to hide or show sub-calendars

Hover over the sub-calendar, an eye icon appears to the right.

[![toggle show hide subcalendar](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![toggle show hide subcalendar](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/toggle-show-hide-subcalendar.gif)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/toggle-show-hide-subcalendar.gif)

Click to show events on this sub-calendar only. All other sub-calendars become invisible. If expected events are not visible, make sure the sub-calendars are not hidden.

<figure aria-describedby="caption-attachment-6288" class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_6288" style="width: 1280px">[![toggle to show events on subcalendar](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![toggle to show events on subcalendar](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/toggle-to-show-events-on-subcalendar.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/toggle-to-show-events-on-subcalendar.png)<figcaption class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-6288">Toggle to show events on the selected sub-calendar. If any events are also assigned to other calendars, those calendars remain invisible. Click to see a large image.</figcaption></figure>Learn more about [hiding or showing sub-calendars](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/hide-calendars/).

#### **Filter by sub-calendars** 

Go to the Filter section below the calendar list. Click the *by sub-calendar* field and select your desired sub-calendar or start typing a word that’s in your calendar <span style="font-weight: 400;">into the </span>*<span style="font-weight: 400;">keyword</span>*<span style="font-weight: 400;"> field and </span>then select from the shortened list.

<figure aria-describedby="caption-attachment-6289" class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_6289" style="width: 1280px">[![filter by subcalendar](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![filter by subcalendar](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/filter-by-subcalendar.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/filter-by-subcalendar.png)<figcaption class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-6289">Use Filter to display events on the selected sub-calendar only. If any events are also assigned to other sub-calendars, those sub-calendars are visible through the color stripes. Click to see a larger image.</figcaption></figure>Read more about [filtering events](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-filter-events-to-display-only-what-you-want-to-see/).
