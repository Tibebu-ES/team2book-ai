In any calendar view with a [time grid](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/ways-to-quickly-move-events/#option-1-drag-and-drop-in-a-grid-view), you can drag and drop to reschedule events. In Year view, you can adjust to show the months needed, then quickly drag and drop to reschedule events over days, weeks, or months. Try it out on [the Year view live demo](https://teamup.com/ksr4dairbp41qnnzhn).

1. Use your mouse to click and hold on any event you want to move.
2. Drag it to the new date and “drop” it in place by releasing the mouse button.

### Adjust the months and dates shown

Focus in on the relevant time period for rescheduling events. You can adjust the date and choose to show from 1 to 12 months in Year view. So if you want to focus on quarterly planning or see a single project’s timeline, you can set the Year view accordingly.

### Toggle sub-calendars on and off from view

You can also choose [which sub-calendars show](https://blog.teamup.com/2018/06/05/quick-tip-show-or-hide-sub-calendars-with-a-click/) to work with selected events. If you need to focus on rescheduling meetings for the next few months, toggle the Meetings sub-calendar on and leave all others off to see only the scheduled meetings.

Learn more about [quickly moving events](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/ways-to-quickly-move-events/) or [long-term planning with Year view](https://blog.teamup.com/2018/04/04/looking-big-picture-year-view/).
