--------------------------

Different schemes of counting the weeks of a year are in common use today. This is due to the fact that different regions in the world use a different definition of what a week is. While, for example, the United States, Canada, Brazil, Japan and other countries consider Sunday as the first day of the week, and while the week begins with Saturday in much of the Middle East, the international [ISO 8601 standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_week_date#Other_week_numbering_systems) and most of Europe has Monday as the first day of the week.

Teamup supports the two most commonly used schemes to count weeks. The scheme used is based on the week start day setting:

- ISO 8610 is used if the week start day is set to any day except Sunday.
- US System is used if the week start day is set to Sunday.

### Set the start day for the week

The calendar administrator can configure the week start day:

1. Open Teamup in a browser.
2. Go to **Settings &gt; Date &amp; Time.**
3. Scroll to the **Week Starts On** section.
4. Choose the start day from the menu.
5. Your change will be saved automatically.

### Week numbering based on ISO 8610

The international standard ISO 8610 defines Monday as the first day of the week and Sunday as the last day of the week. Week number 1 is the week that contains January 4.

**The ISO week numbering scheme is activated if the week start day is set to any day except Sunday.**

### Week numbering based on the US System

In the US, Sunday is commonly viewed as the first day of the week and Saturday the last day of the week. Week number 1 is the week that contains January 1.

**The US week numbering scheme is activated if the week start day is set to Sunday.**
