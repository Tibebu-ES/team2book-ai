 </nav> </div><div class="hkb-article__content" itemprop="text">Table view is a unique and powerful view that lets you view and sort your calendar data in a spreadsheet-like format. You can choose to show or hide columns in Table view so that you only see exactly what you need to see.

### From the Show/Hide Column list

[![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/show-hide-columns-table-viewNEW.gif)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/show-hide-columns-table-viewNEW.gif)

1. Switch to Table view in the top right-hand corner.
2. Click *Show/Hide Columns.*
3. Tick or untick the columns.

### From the individual column menu

You can also hide columns by using the drop-down menu on the individual column. When you’re ready to show the column again, tick it in the Show/Hide Columns list:

[![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/show-hide-individual-columns-teamupNEW.gif)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/show-hide-individual-columns-teamupNEW.gif)

Try it out yourself on our [live demo calendar ](https://teamup.com/ksmxbfc4qf2cwrswxe)or your own calendar!
