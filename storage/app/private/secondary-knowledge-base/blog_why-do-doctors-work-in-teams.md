Realities About Team-based Care
-------------------------------

[Across Quebec and other provinces, more doctors prefer working in a team](https://www.fmoq.org/guide-pratique-fmoq/lexique/partage-des-roles-et-responsabilites-au-sein-de-lequipe/). Such facilities constitute a wide range of professionals, including physicians, nurses, psychologists, social workers, physiotherapists, and other essential healthcare workers.

It offers patients access to the right care based on their present condition and medical history. Therefore, when a patient requires medical attention, seeing a doctor may not always be the first step.

Instead, a psychologist or a physiotherapist may be best suited to handle a particular issue. Nevertheless, physicians still remain relevant in overall healthcare service delivery without overburdening them with unnecessary consultations, which can be handled by other professionals.

Likewise, patients receive immediate care with minimal delay.

Benefits of Teamwork in Patient Care
------------------------------------

Schemes in Quebec offer various benefits to healthcare professionals and patients. Here are some benefits to consider:

- It enhances the overall quality of service offered by healthcare professionals to patients through effective collaboration among doctors, nurses, and other experts.
- It expands continuity and access to healthcare services, which minimises any bottlenecks that may exist. As such, it allows patients to receive care from different professionals within a typical clinic or healthcare facility.
- It allows doctors to prioritise consultations and possible surgeries while other experts concentrate on preparatory tasks, monitoring, and continuous education. Through teamwork, quality is maintained without limiting access for patients.

How Healthcare Professionals Work in Teams
------------------------------------------

In general, there are two ways healthcare professionals can operate as a team. Each framework is different, offers specific benefits, but also presents different challenges.

### Appointment-based Teamwork

Appointment-based teamwork in Quebec primarily occurs through Family Medicine Groups (FMG). It involves a patient passing through various appointments based on their specific health conditions.

For example, a patient may first see a physician. Next, they may return for another appointment with a specialist consultant for a specific diagnosis or proceed to a pathologist to run specific tests that confirm or rule out a diagnosis.

In this model, each professional sees a patient based on a recommendation by a doctor. As such, coordinated team care is delivered at each appointment.

Appointment-based teamwork within a clinic is straightforward but requires effective scheduling solutions like Team2Book to maximise efficiency and minimise overcrowding.

### Pair-based Consultations

[Pair-based consultation](https://youtu.be/xbCtWggTLC8) is the second type of teamwork among healthcare professionals. In this situation, doctors work in pairs with licensed practical nurses or clinical assistants (often medical students or resident physicians) who welcome patients and take vital signs, speeding up the entire appointment.

On the other hand, the doctor or physician prioritises diagnosis or treatment, which reduces overall wait time. In some cases, an assistant can help multiple doctors prepare patients before their consultation.

Although this method can be efficient, scheduling is mostly complex and requires meticulous planning. Some of the challenges of pair-based consultations include:

- At least a doctor and an assistant must be present during a patient’s appointment.
- Each professional requires some space to perform their task.
- An assistant can be overwhelmed when they have to pair with more than a doctor, which can negatively affect efficiency.
- A doctor paired with an assistant may require multiple spaces as they may need to shift from one examination room to another to consult with patients.

Manually managing such a clinic through pair-based consultation is challenging and often leads to mistakes. Leveraging a tool like the Team2Book Availability Scheduling App helps maintain fairness and efficiency.
