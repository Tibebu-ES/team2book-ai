### What the numbers mean

Uptime data is collected by four monitoring servers located in Australia, Asia, Europe and the United States. These monitoring servers periodically submit requests to our servers and track the responses. The monitoring servers are located outside of Teamup’s data centers and access the service across the Internet, like a regular calendar user.

The reported uptime numbers reflect any kind of downtime, including planned maintenance, unplanned maintenance, network downtime, etc. We believe these numbers give you a realistic picture of the reliability you can expect on average from Teamup.

In our experience, the most significant source of downtime is network connectivity issues between users’ computers and the Teamup servers, followed by planned maintenance (which we usually schedule outside of main business hours) and unplanned server issues.
