 </nav> </div><div class="hkb-article__content" itemprop="text">When you no longer need access to a particular calendar, you can remove it from your dashboard. However, removing a calendar from your dashboard is not the same as canceling a calendar’s subscription or deleting an entire calendar. If a calendar has an active subscription, the subscription will **not** be canceled by removing the calendar from your dashboard. First, cancel the subscription. Then, remove the calendar from your dashboard.

Cancel a calendar’s subscription
--------------------------------

To cancel a calendar’s subscription, access the calendar’s settings (on a browser). In the Subscription section, you can [cancel the subscription](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-cancel-subscription-plans/) and [remove payment details](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/managing-subscription-plans/#remove-payment-method).

[▶️ View an interactive demo](https://www.teamup.com/learn/interactive-demos/how-to-cancel-a-calendar-subscription/)

1. Open Teamup in a browser. Make sure you have the right calendar open.  
    *The subscription for each calendar can only be managed in that calendar’s settings by the administrator.*
2. Click the blue menu.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Select **Subscription** from the left sidebar.
5. Click **Cancel Subscription** to confirm.
6. **Confirm** subscription cancelation.
7. Details on the cancellation can be found in the notes section.

Remove calendar from dashboard
------------------------------

Your user **[dashboard](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/add-calendar-to-dashboard/)** contains all the calendars you created or were added to as a user. When you no longer want a particular calendar on your dashboard, you can remove it.

If you are a calendar administrator and wish to cancel a calendar’s subscription and remove it from your dashboard, first follow the steps above to cancel the subscription.

Then proceed with removing the calendar from your dashboard:

[▶️ View an interactive demo](https://www.teamup.com/learn/interactive-demos/remove-a-calendar-from-your-dashboard/)

1. Open Teamup in a browser. Make sure you are logged in.
2. Click your **avatar**, then click **Dashboard**.
3. **Hover over the calendar tile** you want to remove.
4. Click **Remove**.
5. When prompted, enter your user account [password](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/using-teamup-user-account/#change-or-reset-password).
6. On the next screen, click **Remove** to confirm your choice.
7. The calendar will be removed from your dashboard.

If you get an error message when trying to remove a calendar from your dashboard, [see this article](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/remove-a-calendar-from-your-dashboard/#unable-to-remove-calendar) to resolve the error.

Alternately, if you are the calendar owner and no longer wish to keep the calendar for archival purposes, see how to [delete an entire calendar](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/delete-calendars/).
