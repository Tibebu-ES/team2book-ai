What’s included for free
------------------------

A free Teamup calendar is a fully functional calendar which can be synced with the free Teamup mobile app.

Here is an overview of what is included in a free Teamup calendar:

- Up to 5 color-coded [sub-calendars](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-is-a-sub-calendar/)
- [Folders](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-are-folders/) for sub-calendars
- Up to 5 [users](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-share-your-teamup-calendar-users-groups-links/)
- 1 [custom event field](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-are-event-fields/)
- 1 year of [historical data](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/access-historical-data/)
- [Notifications](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/managing-notifications/) for changes in future events
- [iCalendar feeds](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-icalendar-feeds/)
- Advanced [permissions](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-are-access-permissions/)
- [Branding](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/customizing-logos-and-brands/) with your logo
- Assign one event to [multiple sub-calendars](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/assign-one-event-to-multiple-calendars/)
- Multiple [calendar views](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-are-calendar-views/)
- [Recurring events](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-is-a-recurring-event/)
- Unique [Scheduler](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/scheduler-view/) and Yearly view
- [Social sharing](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-are-the-event-sharing-options/)
- Share event [as a webpage](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/share-calendar-events-web-pages/)
- [Event comments](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/event-comments/)
- [Event signups](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/signup-for-an-event/)
- No [double booking](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/double-booking-reservations/)
- [Time zone support](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/time-zone-support/)

To see a full breakdown of what is included in each subscription level, see [here](https://www.teamup.com/pricing/).

Create a free Teamup calendar
-----------------------------

To get started using Teamup as a free service, go [here](http://teamup.com/calendars) and create a free Teamup master calendar.

- If you do not yet have a Teamup user account, you will be prompted to create one.
- Then you can [log in](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-are-user-accounts/) to your new Teamup user account and access your calendar from your [dashboard](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-is-the-calendar-dashboard/).

Open Teamup in a browser to access the calendar settings. This is where you can add new sub-calendars, add users, create shareable links, and more.

Click the blue menu in the top right, then click Settings:  
[![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/241007tu-calendar-settings.gif)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/241007tu-calendar-settings.gif)

In the calendar settings, you can configure your calendar with sub-calendars, folders, calendar views, and more. See [here](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-are-the-calendar-settings/) to learn about the settings.

Create a free Teamup user account
---------------------------------

Teamup calendars can be synced between all your devices if you have a Teamup user account. This makes our free Teamup calendar apps even better!

Register [here](https://teamup.com/register/) to create a free Teamup user account. Then you can login to access your calendar dashboard. Learn more about Teamup users accounts [here](https://teamup.com/register/).

You can add and remove calendars to your dashboard as desired. See [here](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-is-the-calendar-dashboard/) for details. Any calendar that is on your dashboard will stay synced between devices, as long as you log into your Teamup user account.

Use the free Teamup mobile apps
-------------------------------

<div class="wp-block-image"><figure class="alignright">![Teamup has free calendar app for iOS and Android](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![Teamup has free calendar app for iOS and Android](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/ebcmockup2.png)</noscript></figure></div>Teamup also offers free mobile apps for both [iOS](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-teamup-app-4-iphone-ipad/) and [Android](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/using-mobile-app-android/).

Download the free Teamup calendar app you wish to use:

- for [iOS](https://itunes.apple.com/app/teamup-calendar/id1065897968)
- for [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.teamup.teamup)

Then log in to your user account when prompted. You will then see your calendar dashboard with your synced calendar(s). When you update an event on your mobile device, it will sync to your browser, and vice versa.

Share your Teamup calendar
--------------------------

With Teamup’s free service, you can share your calendar with up to 5 users (account users or links).

You can also share individual events from your free Teamup calendar. There are built-in sharing options for social media, other calendar services, email, and event pages: see [here](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/share-events-customize-sharing-options/) for details. Teamup mobile apps also have built-in sharing options for social media, other apps, and event pages: see [this article](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/sharing-options-on-teamup-mobile-apps/).
