Here’s how to install Teamup app widgets on Android devices. See also how to install Teamup app widgets on [iOS devices](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-install-widgets-for-the-teamup-ios-app/#install-the-teamup-app-for-ios).

Install the Teamup app for Android
----------------------------------

Make sure you have downloaded the latest version of the Teamup app for Android.

Open the Google Play store and **[download or update the Android app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.teamup.teamup)** to your device.

[![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/teamup-android-app-download.png)</noscript>](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.teamup.teamup)

You can also find the Teamup Calendar app by searching for *Teamup Solutions AG* on the Google Play store.

Log into your Teamup user account so you can access your calendars. See detailed instructions [here](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-teamup-app-4-iphone-ipad/#connect-the-ios-app-to-your-calendars).

Add a widget
------------

[![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/widget11.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/widget11.png)

There are two ways to add a widget to your phone:

- **Option 1:** Long press the Teamup app icon on your home screen. A menu will appear. Tap the Widgets option.
- **Option 2:** Long press anywhere on the screen to show the options for Wallpapers, Widgets, and Home Settings. Select Widgets and scroll through the available widgets until you find the Teamup widget. Touch and hold to select the widget, then drag it to where you want to place it on your screen.

Configure the widget
--------------------

Once you place the widget on your phone’s screen, you can select the calendar and the sub-calendars you want to display on the widget.

- The default configuration is to mirror the calendar that is displayed when you open the Teamup app on your device. You can change the default and set the widget to display another calendar:
- To change which calendar appears in the widget, tap the *Edit* icon in the top bar of the widget.
- You can add as many widgets as you need and choose a different calendar source for each widget.

To adjust the size of the widget, long press it until a white border with handles appears around the widget. Then drag the handles to adjust the size.
