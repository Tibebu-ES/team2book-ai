<meta content="March 5, 2021" itemprop="datePublished"></meta> <header class="hkb-article__header">How to Copy or Move Events to Today
===================================

 </header></head><body><div class="hkb-article__content" itemprop="text">You can quickly move any event to the current date.

1. Open your calendar on a web browser and right-click any event to open the context menu.
2. Choose the **Move to** or **Copy to** option.
3. Click the **Today** button that appears at the bottom of the pop-up calendar to move or copy the event to the current day.

The event will be moved or copied to the same time slot or remain as an All-day event. Alternatively, drag and drop to move or ctrl-drag and drop to copy. See the shortcut in action below:
