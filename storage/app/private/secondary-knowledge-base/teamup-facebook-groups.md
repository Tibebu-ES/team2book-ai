<meta content="October 19, 2014" itemprop="datePublished"></meta> <header class="hkb-article__header">How to Use Teamup Calendar Inside Facebook Groups
=================================================

 </header></head><body><div class="hkb-article__content" itemprop="text">Here are some creative ways to share events from a Teamup Calendar within a Facebook Group:

- Add a post with your calendar link (a read-only link is recommended) and pin it so it stays at the top of the page.
- Periodically post your calendar link to promote it to new group members. The link is accessible on mobile devices, however, the calendar opens outside Facebook.
- Add a direct link to your Teamup Calendar in the About section of your group
- Take a screenshot of your upcoming events and post it along with your calendar link.
- **[Post events](http://calendar.teamup.com/kb/share-calendar-events-web-pages/)** one at a time: Open an event or right-click to open the context menu, click Share &gt; As page, then copy the page URL and post it to your timeline.
- **[More about sharing](http://calendar.teamup.com/kb/share-event-social-media-facebook-twitter/)** on social media.

If you have any other ways to share your calendar with your Facebook groups, please let us know and we’ll share them further.
