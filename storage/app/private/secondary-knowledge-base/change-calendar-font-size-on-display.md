 </nav> </div><div class="hkb-article__content" itemprop="text">When viewing a Teamup Calendar in some [calendar views](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-calendar-views/), including day, week, month, or multi-week, the size of the blocks on the calendar grid are dependent on your screen size and resolution.

In some cases, such as when you are viewing your calendar on a small screen or on a screen with a high resolution, **the font can become difficult to read** as it has a very small space to be displayed in.

While there is no specific Teamup Calendar setting to increase the font size, you can **use your computer’s zoom functionality to change the font size:**

### On all devices

- **Go to your browser’s view menu** and choose the zoom in or out options. See the **[instructions for all browsers](https://www.wikihow.com/Zoom-With-a-Web-Browser)**.

### On a **[Windows](https://www.wikihow.com/Zoom-Out-on-a-PC)** device

- **Press ctrl and ‘+’ together to zoom in**. Press ctrl and ‘-‘ to zoom out. A zoom status box appears in the top right for a few seconds where you can press + or – to adjust further. Pressing ctrl and ‘0’ together resets your window to normal zoom.

### On an **[Apple Mac](https://www.wikihow.com/Zoom-out-on-a-Mac)**

- **Press Command and ‘+’ together to zoom in**. Pressing Command and ‘-‘ will zoom out. Command and ‘0’ will reset your zoom level.

For both Windows and Mac, each key press repetition will increase the zoom by several degrees. You can zoom in to varying degrees in different browsers. Experiment with zooming on your device and browser, and see what works best for you.
