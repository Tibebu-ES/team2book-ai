 </nav> </div><div class="hkb-article__content" itemprop="text">Teamup’s [subscription plans](https://www.teamup.com/pricing/) are [calendar-based](https://teamup.com/event/show/id/U6zjMotpg5iNKZBuottajcWK4D69ZW): Each subscription plan is bound to a specific master calendar. Only a [calendar administrator](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-is-an-administrator-link/) can cancel a calendar’s subscription. Subscription changes are done via the calendar [settings](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-are-the-calendar-settings/) (browser only).

Change a calendar’s subscription
--------------------------------

**For all subscription changes, start here:**

1. Open the calendar in a browser.
2. Click the **blue menu** (top right).
3. Select **Settings.**
4. On the **Subscription** tab, you will see information about the current subscription plan:

[![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/251203tu-pricing-settings-page-monthly0-1024x576.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/251203tu-pricing-settings-page-monthly0.png)

### Upgrade from free to a paid subscription

1. Follow the steps [above](#steps).
2. In the Billing section, **click the blue Billing details button** and enter your payment information, then **Save**.
3. Click the **Monthly** or **Yearly tab** to select your billing cycle.
4. Click the **green Select button** for the plan you want.

### Downgrade from paid to free

1. Follow the steps [above](#steps).
2. Click **Cancel Subscription** in the top right of the screen.
3. Confirm your choice on the next screen.
4. Scroll to the **Billing section**.
5. Click the **trash can icon** next to the Update Payment Method button.
6. On the next screen, click **Delete Payment Method** to confirm.

### Downgrade a paid subscription

1. Follow the steps [above](#steps).
2. Click the **Monthly** or **Yearly tab** to select your billing cycle. The plan pricing will change to reflect the Monthly or Yearly charge for each plan.
3. Click the **green Select button** for the plan you want.

[The downgrade](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/managing-subscription-plans/#downgrade) will apply at the end of your current billing cycle.

### Upgrade a paid subscription

1. Follow the steps [above](#steps).
2. Click the **Monthly** or **Yearly tab** to select your billing cycle. The plan pricing will change to reflect the Monthly or Yearly charge for each plan.
3. Click the **green Select button** for the plan you want.
4. A pop-up will open to show you a preview of the price difference for the upgrade. **Confirm** the upgrade.
5. Once confirmed, you will be charged for the difference in price and the upgrade will be applied to your calendar immediately.

**ℹ️ [Learn more](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/managing-subscription-plans/#subscription-plans) about managing subscription plans.**
