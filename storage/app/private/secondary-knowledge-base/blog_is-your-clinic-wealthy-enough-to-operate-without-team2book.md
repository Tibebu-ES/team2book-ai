How Much Does a Scheduling Staff Cost?
--------------------------------------

Regardless of the size of your clinic or income, you would have to spend at least **$60,000 CAD** for a medical scheduling coordinator who would manage scheduling and resource allocation. Clinics that want to save money may assign this task to a doctor or nurse with other responsibilities, thinking scheduling is not a big deal, will quickly start to see missed shifts, double bookings, and much more.

For smaller medical clinics, schedule coordination is typically performed by the clinic manager in addition to their other responsibilities. A clinic manager may earn an average annual salary of **$80,000** CAD.

These administrative staff generally perform the following tasks:

- Fairly assign office spaces to medical professionals with minimal bias.
- Coordinate on-call, night, weekend, and special clinic shifts among staff.
- Track changes and make adjustments to different calendars when required.
- Inform and remind staff about the appointments/assignments.
- Avoid overstaffing and understaffing.
- Ensure adequate cover for all shifts.
- Track statistics to improve overall workflow efficiency and transparency.
