For example, if the calendar title is “Acme Company Calendar” and you are printing a meeting agenda, you may want to show a heading such as “Quarterly Meeting Agenda” as the title of the printout. Or if you are printing a route schedule for bus drivers, you may wish to show “Bus 17” as the heading, for example.

Set a custom title on calendar printouts
----------------------------------------

You can use a [calendar link parameter](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/calendar-link-parameters/) to temporarily change the displayed calendar title. This lets you print the schedule with the desired title.

- In a browser, [display the schedule](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-quickly-print-a-filtered-calendar/) that you wish to print.
- Click into your browser’s URL bar.
- Append the following to your calendar link: **?title=Your Custom Title**
- Click the blue hamburger menu (top right), select **PDF Print &amp; Export**, and [print the schedule](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/print-a-calendar/).
- For additional printed schedules, change the title in the calendar parameter to whatever you need it to be.
- When done with printing, remove the calendar parameter and refresh your browser to see your calendar’s usual title once more. See [here](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/calendar-link-parameters/) for more on calendar parameters.
