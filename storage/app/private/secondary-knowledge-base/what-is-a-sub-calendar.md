<meta content="November 20, 2019" itemprop="datePublished"></meta> <header class="hkb-article__header">What is a sub-calendar?
=======================

 </header></head><body><div class="hkb-article__content" itemprop="text">Sub-calendars are individual calendars that live within your master Teamup calendar.

Sub-calendars are individual calendars that are contained within [your master calendar](http://blog.teamup.com/2018/06/20/whats-the-difference-between-a-master-calendar-and-sub-calendars/). You can organize sub-calendars in [folders](http://blog.teamup.com/2018/07/17/what-are-folders/), and toggle folders or individual sub-calendars [on and off](http://blog.teamup.com/2018/06/05/quick-tip-show-or-hide-sub-calendars-with-a-click/) in your calendar view.

You’ll find your sub-calendars in a list on the left sidebar of your calendar (in a browser):
