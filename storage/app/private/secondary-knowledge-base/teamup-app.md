Download the Teamup app
-----------------------

### Teamup app for iOS

Go to the App Store and **[download the iOS app](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/teamup-calendar/id1065897968)** to your device.

[![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/download_on_the_app_store.png)</noscript>](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/teamup-calendar/id1065897968)

You can also find the Teamup Calendar app by searching for *Teamup Solutions AG* on the App Store.

### Teamup app for Android

Open the Google Play store and **[download the Android app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.teamup.teamup)** to your device.

[![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/teamup-android-app-download.png)</noscript>](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.teamup.teamup)

You can also find the Teamup Calendar app by searching for *Teamup Solutions AG* on the Google Play store.

Get started
-----------

Important note: The Teamup mobile app is a companion product of the web-based Teamup Calendar. On the app, you can access your Teamup calendars, work with events, and manage app settings. To create a new Teamup calendar or access the calendar settings, use a browser.

→ [See the full Teamup app tutorial](https://www.teamup.com/apps/)

### Log in or sign up

It’s easiest and most secure to use the Teamup app with a free user account.

#### If you already have a Teamup account

[![Log into the Teamup app by tapping Login, tap Continue to sign in, then enter your login credentials. ](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![Log into the Teamup app by tapping Login, tap Continue to sign in, then enter your login credentials. ](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/240816tum-app-login-1024x576.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/240816tum-app-login.png)

1. Tap **Login.**
2. Tap **Continue** on the pop-up asking for permission.
3. Enter your email and tap **Continue**.
4. Enter your password and tap **Log in**.
5. If you have 2FA set up, enter an authorization code when prompted.
6. Your dashboard will open. Tap any calendar tile to open it.

#### If you don’t have a Teamup account

[![On the Teamup app, you can sign up for an account: Tap the Sign Up button, enter your name and email, choose a password, then confirm your email address.](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![On the Teamup app, you can sign up for an account: Tap the Sign Up button, enter your name and email, choose a password, then confirm your email address.](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/240816tum-app-signup-1024x576.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/240816tum-app-signup.png)

1. Tap **Sign up**.
2. Enter your name and email and choose a password.
3. Check your email. Tap the link in the activation message.
4. Now you can **Log in** on the Teamup app.
5. Your dashboard will open. Tap any calendar tile to open it.

#### Continue without an account

You can use the Teamup app without an account. You will still have a dashboard, but it will not synchronize across devices or platforms.

[![You can use the Teamup app without an account by tapping the blue Continue without account option on the introductory screen.](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![You can use the Teamup app without an account by tapping the blue Continue without account option on the introductory screen.](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/240816tum-app-continue-no-account-1024x576.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/240816tum-app-continue-no-account.png)

1. Tap **Continue without account**.
2. Enter a calendar link or tap **try a demo calendar**.
3. Tap **Continue**.
4. Your dashboard will open. Tap the calendar tile to open it.
5. To connect more calendars, follow the steps below.

<a name="connect"></a>Connect calendars
---------------------------------------

If you have account-based access to any Teamup calendars, they will automatically be added to your dashboard once you log in.

If you have calendar links, you can add them to your dashboard.

[![To add a calendar to the Teamup app, go to the Dashboard and tap the green + button, then choose Connect an existing calendar, then paste or type in the calendar link.](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![To add a calendar to the Teamup app, go to the Dashboard and tap the green + button, then choose Connect an existing calendar, then paste or type in the calendar link.](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/240816tum-app-connect-calendar-1024x576.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/240816tum-app-connect-calendar.png)

1. Swipe to open the right sidebar.
2. Tap **Dashboard**.
3. Tap the **green plus ⊕.**
4. Choose **Connect an existing calendar**.
5. Type or paste in the calendar link and tap **Continue**.
6. Your calendar will show up on your dashboard. Tap to open the calendar.

App navigation and settings
---------------------------

### Left sidebar

[![Tap or swipe to open the left sidebar on the Teamup app](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![Tap or swipe to open the left sidebar on the Teamup app](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/240816tum-app-open-left-sidebar.gif)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/240816tum-app-open-left-sidebar.gif)

#### See calendar information

1. 1. Swipe or tap to open the left sidebar.
    2. The calendar title, logo, and user or link name appear in the blue section at the top.
    3. You can tap the calendar logo to open the Dashboard.

#### Refresh the calendar

1. 1. Swipe or tap to open the left sidebar.
    2. The time since last update and the refresh arrow appear in the blue section at the top, under the calendar information.
    3. Tap the **arrow ↻** to manually refresh your calendar.

→ [More details](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-refresh-works-on-teamup-mobile-apps/).

#### Access app settings

In the app settings, you can change the time zone, manage change notifications, choose whether to display empty days, open the calendar in a mobile browser, and remove the calendar from your dashboard.

1. 1. Swipe or tap to open the left sidebar.
    2. Tap the **gear ⚙** in the blue section at the top to open app settings.

Reminder: To access [calendar settings](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/what-are-the-calendar-settings/), open the calendar in a browser with administrator access.

#### Switch calendar views

1. 1. Swipe or tap to open the left sidebar.
    2. To switch calendar views, tap on **List, Day, Week, Month, or Tiles**.

→ [More details](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-calendar-views-on-teamup-mobile-apps/).

#### Toggle sub-calendars

[![Toggle from the calendar list to show or hide sub-calendars on the Teamup app.](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![Toggle from the calendar list to show or hide sub-calendars on the Teamup app.](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/show-or-hide-sub-calendars-iOS-simple.gif)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/show-or-hide-sub-calendars-iOS-simple.gif)

Toggle sub-calendars to show only events on the selected sub-calendars. This is especially helpful with busy calendars, as it declutters the view and makes it easy to focus on relevant events.

1. 1. Swipe or tap to open the left sidebar.
    2. Tap the **eye icon** to select one sub-calendar, all sub-calendars in a folder, or all sub-calendars.
    3. Tap the title bar of a sub-calendar to add or remove it from the selection.

### Right sidebar

[![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/240905tum-app-right-sidebar-resize.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/240905tum-app-right-sidebar-resize.png)

#### See profile information

1. 1. Swipe or tap to open the right sidebar.
    2. Your user name, email, and online status appear in the blue section at the top.

#### Open the dashboard

1. 1. Swipe or tap to open the right sidebar.
    2. Tap **Dashboard**.

#### Contact support

1. 1. Swipe or tap to open the right sidebar.
    2. Tap **Feedback**.
    3. A pre-populated email with your device and app info will open in your email client. Add your questions or comments before sending so we know how to help you.

#### Log out

1. 1. Swipe or tap to open the right sidebar.
    2. Tap **Log out**.
    3. On the confirmation pop-up, tap **Log out**.

#### Delete account

1. 1. Swipe or tap to open the right sidebar.
    2. Tap **Delete account**.
    3. You will be prompted to log in on a mobile browser and confirm that you wish to delete your account.

Working with events
-------------------

#### View events

- Tap an event to view its details.
- Files attached to the event will appear as clickable links so that you can open them directly from your mobile device.
- Tap an address in the **Where** field to open it in your map app.
- [Images](http://calendar.teamup.com/kb/add-images-event-details/) in the **Description** field can be displayed in the event view as shown below.
- Images and files in the **Attachments** field can be [previewed](https://teamup.com/event/show/id/ZmDYbZayznKoyhsZKRBnZQ5t71TQRB) in the app.

[![The event editor on the Teamup app is fully functional. Use markdown to format text in the description, upload images, include hyperlinks, use signups and comments, and more.](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![The event editor on the Teamup app is fully functional. Use markdown to format text in the description, upload images, include hyperlinks, use signups and comments, and more.](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/kb-1-iphone-03.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/kb-1-iphone-03.png)

#### Create a new event

1. Tap the **green plus ⊕** (bottom right). Or tap anywhere in the time grid.
2. The event editor will open. Enter the event title, adjust the time, and add other information as needed: 
    - Make the event [repeating](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/recurring-events/#on-teamup-mobile-apps).
    - [Use Markdown](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-format-the-event-description-on-teamup-mobile-apps/) to format text in the description field.
    - Enable [event signups](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-event-signup-on-teamup-mobile-apps/).
    - Use [event comments](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-comments-on-teamup-mobile-apps/).
    - [Upload](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-attach-files-to-events/#upload-on-teamup-mobile-apps) images or files**.**
3. Tap the **check ✓** (top right) to save the new event.

#### Edit events

1. Tap the event to open it.
2. Tap **the pencil** (bottom right) to edit the event. If you do not have [the appropriate access permission](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/share-calendars-access-permissions/) to edit events, the pencil icon will not be shown.
3. Adjust the title, date, time, or other fields as needed: 
    - The [Description field](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-format-the-event-description-on-teamup-mobile-apps/).
    - [Recurrence rules](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/recurring-events/#on-teamup-mobile-apps).
    - [Event signups](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-event-signup-on-teamup-mobile-apps/).
    - [Event comments](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-comments-on-teamup-mobile-apps/).
    - [Uploaded images or files.](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-attach-files-to-events/#upload-on-teamup-mobile-apps)
4. Tap the **check ✓** (top right) to save your changes.

#### Delete an event

1. Tap the event to open it.
2. Tap **the pencil** (bottom right) to open the event editor.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete event**.
4. On the confirmation pop-up, tap **Delete event**.

#### Share events

1. Tap the event to open it.
2. Tap the **••• menu** (top right).
3. Tap **Share** and choose the option you want from the Share menu.
4. OR tap **Open page** to to generate a stand-alone event page, which you can share.

→ [More details](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/sharing-options-on-teamup-mobile-apps/#how-to-share-an-event).

#### Filter events

[![Filter events on the Teamup app by keywords, sub-calendars, or any custom field filters. ](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![Filter events on the Teamup app by keywords, sub-calendars, or any custom field filters. ](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/240816tum-app-filter-events-compressed.gif)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/240816tum-app-filter-events-compressed.gif)

1. Tap the **🔍 magnifying glass** (top right).
2. Set a single filter or use a combination: 
    1. **Keyword filter**: Enter one or more keywords.
    2. **Custom fields**: Tap the field name, tap the option(s), then tap the check **✓** (top right).
    3. **Sub-calendars**: Tap Sub-calendars, tap the calendar name(s), then tap the check **✓** (top right).
3. Tap the **check ✓** (top right) to see the filtered results.
4. Tap the **X** (top right) to clear the filters.

App widgets
-----------

[![Use widgets to get a quick look at your schedule from the Teamup app. Customize which sub-calendars show in each widget.](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![Use widgets to get a quick look at your schedule from the Teamup app. Customize which sub-calendars show in each widget.](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/240508-tu-mobile-widgets.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/240508-tu-mobile-widgets.png)

Widgets are a convenient, helpful way to display your Teamup calendar on your phone’s home screen and provides a shortcut to your calendar events.

The exact steps may vary a bit, depending on your device and operating system:

1. Long-press on the home screen to open up the menu for widgets.
2. Select Teamup from the available widgets.
3. Choose the layout you want and confirm.
4. Press and hold to drag the widget where you want it on the screen.
5. Tap the edit icon or long-press the widget to show the edit menu to configure which calendar/sub-calendars are displayed in the widget.

Troubleshooting
---------------

#### Login issues (Android device)

Some Android devices may show a “callback error” when trying to log into the Teamup app. To fix it: Go to **Settings &gt; Apps &gt; Teamup**. Under **Set as default**, make sure the option to **Open supported links** is toggled ON. If this does not resolve the error, [please see here](https://teamup.com/event/show/id/vEWK7LVin6JJwkmDShNvPRxCZMwZqX) for additional things to check.

#### Can’t find my calendar link.

The [calendar administrator](https://blog.teamup.com/2018/12/05/what-is-the-calendar-administrator/) manages all shareable calendar links. If you cannot find your calendar link, contact your calendar administrator. If you are the calendar administrator and have lost access to your calendar, see [this article](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/lost-administrator-url/) for help.

#### Calendars aren’t on my dashboard.

You’ve logged in, but your dashboard doesn’t have the calendars you expected. Here are some reasons that may happen:

- You access these calendars with a calendar link. Follow the [steps above](#connect) to connect the calendars to your dashboard on the app.
- Your calendars are connected to a different user account. For example, your work calendars are connected to a user account created with your work email address. If you just signed up using your personal email, the work calendars won’t show up on your dashboard. Instead of creating a new, separate account, add a [secondary email](https://teamup.com/event/show/id/4hGS5QD8rGuB9SM3ZRDfXYv2qTedLh) (e.g. your personal email address) to the initial account (e.g. your work email).

The Welcome Calendar is a helpful calendar added automatically by the Teamup system for new users. It has a few calendar events with the basic info you need to get started. You can keep it as-is, modify it, or remove it from your dashboard if desired.
