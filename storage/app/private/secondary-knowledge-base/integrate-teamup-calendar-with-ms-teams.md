Do you use MS Teams? If so, your Teamup calendar can be easily integrated with your MS Teams environment.

Anyone who is part of the team (either the MS Teams **owner** or a Team **member**, not a guest) can simply add your Teamup Calendar [as a tab](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/built-in-custom-tabs) to your Teams environment:

![6e8d76e13b751ec10cd647f86a656958.png](https://blog.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![6e8d76e13b751ec10cd647f86a656958.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/inline/65032/ec0bc6b7a190272cf3c7aba2e6aa6066a862d020/22ab3f0ff76c2d2dc69043f6b3bc4a17a85d911f/6e8d76e13b751ec10cd647f86a656958.png)</noscript>

Here’s how:

- Go to your MS Teams environment.
- Click the + then select *Website*:

![643cbeb705f330c7dcc52b4a1bc5b152.png](https://blog.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![643cbeb705f330c7dcc52b4a1bc5b152.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/inline/65032/b98cc8825155cfcff129ab20e4ccd6cdabc6f45f/bd52cdd71d37248df71508f9ea72ad79cb052994/643cbeb705f330c7dcc52b4a1bc5b152.png)</noscript>

- Enter a name for the new tab (for example: *Teamup* or *Calendar).*
- Then paste in a shareable link to your Teamup calendar. (See important note below.)

![b487e2f204f62597e08c54377b14160f.png](https://blog.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![b487e2f204f62597e08c54377b14160f.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/inline/65032/90aeb83735613b7d456ba77a46fb45f44a859462/e4766e3ab661162262b0b59aa467a0d5beff3917/b487e2f204f62597e08c54377b14160f.png)</noscript>

Note: A shareable calendar link will have /ks followed by a 16-digit alphanumeric string (the calendar key):![](https://blog.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/calendar-link-URL.png)</noscript>

Be sure to use a Teamup calendar link with an **appropriate permission level** for anyone who can access the MS Teams environment. More details about permission levels [here](http://blog.teamup.com/2018/07/06/what-are-access-permissions/).

Get inspired – Check out [the story of UN Web TV](https://blog.teamup.com/2020/11/23/un-web-tv-shares-live-schedule-with-teamup/#teams) about adding a tab in their Enterprise Teams to display the live schedule, and sharing it with other UN colleagues who are interested in Web TV’s daily webcast coverage.
