 </nav> </div><div class="hkb-article__content" itemprop="text">There are three options for accessing Teamup Calendars on a mobile device: The Teamup app, mobile browsers, and iCalendar feeds.

The Teamup app
--------------

The [Teamup app](https://www.teamup.com/apps/) (iOS, Android) allows you to easily access your Teamup Calendars from your mobile device. If you are new, first use a web browser to [create and configure](https://teamup.com/calendars) the calendar you need. It will appear on [your dashboard](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1emvyh8ezhM_dgl4fJ78rx_zntxyMC9EOMXsHYa7Fw1o/edit#slide=id.g28c694b7b18_0_114) automatically and you can access it from the Teamup app.

One of the biggest advantages of using the apps is that your calendar is even accessible offline. This can be helpful when you are not connected with the live Teamup servers due to lack of Internet access or [connectivity issues](https://blog.teamup.com/2018/04/16/track-your-connection-status-and-refresh-your-data-on-teamup-mobile-apps/) with your device.

Mobile browsers
---------------

Some Teamup Calendar features are only available in the browser version. If you need these features, or prefer using Teamup’s web client, access Teamup in a mobile browser.

[Check out these tips for Android devices](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/access-calendar-mobile-browser/) and [here for iOS devices](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/access-teamup-calendar-in-a-mobile-browser-ios/) to make your mobile browser experience smoother and more productive.

iCalendar feeds
---------------

[Outbound iCalendar feeds](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/subscribe-to-teamup-icalendar-feeds/) allow you to view a Teamup calendar in another calendar service on your mobile device.

You can [subscribe to Teamup iCalendar feeds](http://calendar.teamup.com/kb/subscribe-teamup-calendar-feed-from-other-calendars/ "Subscribe to Teamup iCalendar feeds") via Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Outlook, or another personal calendar. Then you can see your Teamup Calendar events in other calendar apps on your device.
