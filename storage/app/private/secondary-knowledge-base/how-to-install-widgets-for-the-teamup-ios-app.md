 </nav> </div><div class="hkb-article__content" itemprop="text">Widgets are a convenient way to display Teamup on your phone’s home screen and have a shortcut to your calendar events. Here’s how to install Teamup widgets for iOS 14 or higher. For older iOS versions, the old widget (released in 2019) is still available as described [here](https://blog.teamup.com/2019/01/12/new-on-teamup-ios-app-the-widget-is-here/). See also how to install Teamup app widgets for [Android devices](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-teamup-app-widgets-android/).

Install the Teamup app for iOS
------------------------------

First, make sure you have downloaded the latest version of the [Teamup mobile app for iOS](https://itunes.apple.com/app/teamup-calendar/id1065897968).

Go to the App Store and **[download or update the iOS app](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/teamup-calendar/id1065897968)** to your device.

[![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/download_on_the_app_store.png)</noscript>](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/teamup-calendar/id1065897968)

You can also find the Teamup Calendar app by searching for *Teamup Solutions AG* on the App Store.

Log into your Teamup user account so you can access your calendars. See detailed instructions [here](https://calendar.teamup.com/kb/how-to-use-teamup-app-4-iphone-ipad/#connect-the-ios-app-to-your-calendars).

Add a widget
------------

### iPhone

- To add a widget to any page of your iPhone’s home screen: 
    1. Long press on the home screen.
    2. Click the + on the top left.
    3. Type Teamup in the search box, or scroll to locate Teamup in the list:
    4. Swipe to find one of the 5 widget layouts you like and click *Add Widget.* Repeat to add another layout of the widget, if desired.

###  iPad

- To add a Teamup widget on an iPad in Today View: 
    1. Swipe your finger to the right on your home screen until you see Today View.
    2. Long press an empty area in Today View, or tap the Edit button at the bottom of the screen.
    3. Tap the + button in the top left.
    4. Select the Teamup widget from the list on the left.
    5. Swipe to choose the layout you prefer, then tap Add Widget.
    6. Tap Done.
    7. You can also drag a widget from your Home Screen into Today View.
- To add a Teamup widget to your iPad’s home screen: 
    1. Touch and hold an empty area on your home screen.
    2. Tap the + button in the top left.
    3. Select the Teamup widget from the list on the left.
    4. Swipe to choose the widget layout, then tap Add Widget.
    5. Tap Done in the upper-right corner, or just tap your Home Screen.

Configure the widget
--------------------

Once a widget is added, long press it to bring up the options on both iPhone and iPad devices.

The default configuration is to mirror the calendar that is displayed when you open the Teamup app on your device. You can change the default and set the widget to display another calendar:

1. Unselect “Mirror Teamup App.”
2. Click *Change* to choose another calendar from your dashboard.
3. Click *Change* to choose specific sub-calendars to display in the widget.  
    [![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/plugins/native-lazyload/assets/images/placeholder.svg)<noscript>![](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/widget-4-161x300.png)</noscript>](https://calendar.teamup.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/widget-4.png)  
    You can add as many widgets as you need and choose a different calendar source for each widget.
